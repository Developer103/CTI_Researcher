The pattern repeats: unauthenticated WebLogic RCE → PoC proliferation → fielded exploitation → CISA EEO ~18–24 months later. **CVE-2024-21182 is following the exact same timeline.**

DriveSurge has transitioned from targeted payload delivery to **infrastructure-level campaign scaling**, drastically widening the attack surface beyond traditional IOC-blocklist approaches.

**Nanocore RAT Proliferation:** Three new Nanocore IOCs (IP + domain) filed today — this commodity RAT is being rapidly reactivated, likely as a staging tool for ransomware campaigns.
